# Data contract

- **Engine and data pair.** `policyengine[uk]==6.17.2`, which pins policyengine-uk 2.174.1 and certifies the Microcosm UK 2024-25 national build `20261107T020434Z-eeb6093e` (SHA-256 `0a9b77db1c0dee2693cb2d47bad68da34cb662916eee4829942bcd45de581f25`) for it. The bundle certifies the pair; G0 still records it and confirms it for these measures, and until then every figure carries "unvalidated under this engine".
- **Microdata stay private.** No `.h5` file, record-level extract or weight is committed (`.gitignore`, `scripts/check_public_data.py`). `scripts/youth_base_check.py` prints weighted totals for the team only.
- **Columns the measures read:** `age`, `employment_status`, `current_education`, `is_in_approved_training`, `is_in_non_advanced_education`, `employment_income`, `self_employment_income`, `hours_worked`, `universal_credit_reported`, `is_disabled_for_benefits`, `pip_dl_category`, `pip_m_category`, `region`, `rent`, `tenure_type`.
- **Columns the measures need but the data lack:** work history, NEET status and duration, Work Capability Assessment outcome or UC conditionality group, care-leaver status, employer records and hiring flows (the FRS is a cross-section and the build records no job starts). LCWRA on `eeb6093e` is inferred from disability-benefit receipt; builds with Microcosm #1550 store LCW and LCWRA from the FRS's own UC admin record, and `6f918b81` also fits LCWRA households to DWP's paid count (#3). The pinned build stores `is_apprentice` only as a flag drawn at ages 21-24 for the employer NICs apprentice relief (about 214-237 records, about 130k people weighted): fit for a national score of that relief, about 28% short of HMRC (£0.27bn against £0.37bn in 2024-25), and not for describing apprentices, other ages or sub-national areas (#6).
- **Heavy runs.** Run one data-loading job at a time and check available memory first. Prefer reading the needed columns to full simulations.
